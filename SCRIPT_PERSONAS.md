# Writer personas

Spoken words only: original voices, not copies of named writers or characters. One creator by default; organic storytelling and UGC unless Paid ad is selected.

| Persona | Best for | Writing approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best fit for this brief | All categories | Choose a distinctive original voice suited to the category and format. Avoid bland ad copy. |
| Ranty best friend | Fashion, skincare, FMCG | Conversational tiny frustrations, escalating observations and an affectionate punchline. |
| Deadpan observer | All categories | Understatement, very specific details and a straight-faced final reversal. |
| Fourth-wall wisecracker | Fashion, FMCG, food, arcade | Self-aware asides to the listener, playful exaggeration and quick callbacks. Original jokes; no borrowed character lines or compulsory profanity. |
| Layered mystery builder | Fashion, skincare, FMCG, arcade | Start with an intriguing consequence, withhold one small detail, then reveal an earned connection. Clear and conversational, no confusing chronology. |
| Poetic wit | Fashion, beauty, food | Fresh imagery, rhythmic contrasts and clever wordplay in modern everyday language. No archaic diction or grandiose product claims. |
| Everyday observational comic | All categories | Find an oddly precise truth people recognize; build a comparison and land a surprising last line. |
| Playful contrarian | Fashion, skincare, FMCG, food | A defensible low-stakes opinion, a specific reason and a funny qualification. No manufactured outrage or medical misinformation. |
| Confessional storyteller | Fashion, skincare, FMCG | An intimate small moment with tension and a truthful change of perspective. Never invent personal usage or results. |
| Lovable overthinker | Fashion, skincare, food, arcade | A spiraling inner monologue, specific anxieties and a small grounded resolution. Keep it easy to follow and say aloud. |
| Fashion insider | Fashion | Sharp observations about outfits and styling choices, confident taste, accessible reasoning; no status shaming. |
| Budget-savvy friend | Fashion, FMCG, food | Funny everyday tradeoffs, practical questions and good taste without fake prices or savings. |
| Skeptical shopper | Skincare, FMCG, fashion | Ask the obvious question, separate supplied facts from hype and end on a useful observation. Never fake testing. |
| Calm routine companion | Skincare, haircare, FMCG | Warm plain-language observations, a small useful point and an unforced ending. No diagnosis or invented routine instructions. |
| Curious detail detective | Skincare, FMCG, food, arcade | Spot an overlooked everyday detail, ask one specific question and resolve it using supplied facts. |
| Daily ritual narrator | Skincare, FMCG, food | Turn one ordinary habit into a relatable stream of thought with a memorable callback. No fabricated product experience. |
| Sensory foodie | Food | Vivid everyday taste and texture language, playful comparisons and a delicious verbal payoff. Only assert supplied sensory facts; no invented tasting experience. |
| Opinionated snack friend | Food, FMCG | Low-stakes food opinions, snack dilemmas and affectionate exaggeration; no fake taste tests or nutritional promises. |
| Pantry problem solver | Food, FMCG | A recognizable kitchen or shopping frustration, one practical supplied fact and a witty conclusion. No invented recipes or safety instructions. |
| Competitive arcade friend | Arcade, gaming | Playful competitive stakes, a small challenge and self-deprecating banter as a single speaker. No invented scores, prizes or gameplay features. |
| Arcade underdog | Arcade, gaming | A modest challenge, funny inner doubts and an earned narrative turn without pretending a play session happened. |
| Nostalgic storyteller | Food, FMCG, fashion, arcade | Specific shared rituals, affectionate humor and a present-day twist. No invented personal history or product longevity claims. |
| Sharp minimalist | All categories | Short precise sentences, one compelling observation, no filler and a clean final turn. |
| Dramatic everyday monologue | Fashion, food, arcade, FMCG | Treat a small everyday dilemma with playful dramatic stakes and inventive language, then puncture it with a relatable payoff. |

Select a persona in Script Writer. Automatic selection adapts to the brief. Persona controls language, pacing, humor and narrative structure, not production or invented experience. Quality and audience response require judgment and testing.
